---
name: Survey acceptance
description: The user's updated acceptance criteria for quiz activities in this project.
---

Existing surveys are acceptable quiz activities, and there is no fixed question-count requirement. Preserve their current content and meanings; the goal is to make sure they work, not to convert them into conventional scored quizzes.

**Why:** The user explicitly changed the quiz requirement and asked that existing surveys remain unchanged while functionality is verified.

**How to apply:** When verifying or improving quiz flows, test the current survey activities as they are. Ask before changing survey content, meaning, or database data.
