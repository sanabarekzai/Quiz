---
name: Community results board
description: The user's Phase 4 direction for sharing student quiz and survey outcomes.
---

The user wants the leaderboard to work as a non-competitive, newest-first community results feed. Show usernames, other students' feedback/preferences, and scores only when an activity actually has a score. Include Study Tool Matcher rankings, but exclude Student Wellness results from the shared feed; retain Wellness results in private history. Keep private reflection answers hidden and do not rank students or present winners.

Only signed-in students may view the community board; do not expose it to anonymous visitors.

**Why:** The user wants students to compare useful outcomes and others' preferences, not compete, while keeping the results within the student sign-in experience.

**How to apply:** Require authentication for the board and its API. Keep shared results chronological and username-attributed, hide email addresses, treat opinion surveys and matcher outcomes as ungraded, omit Student Wellness, and preserve private reflection answers.
