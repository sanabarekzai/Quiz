name: Community results board
description: The user's Phase 4 direction for sharing student quiz and survey outcomes.
---

The user wants the leaderboard to work as a non-competitive, newest-first community results feed. Show usernames, other students' feedback/preferences, and scores only when an activity actually has a score. Exclude Student Wellness results from the shared feed; retain them in each student's private history. Keep private reflection answers hidden and do not rank students or present winners.

**Why:** The user wants students to compare useful outcomes and others' preferences, not compete.

**How to apply:** Keep shared results chronological and username-attributed, hide email addresses, treat opinion surveys as ungraded, omit Student Wellness, and preserve private reflection answers.
